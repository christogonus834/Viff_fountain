FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission requirements? ▼
Students must present their previous school's report card/leaving certificate, birth certificate, and sit an entrance assessment. Admission forms are available at the school office.
When is the next academic session? ▼
Our academic year runs September to July, divided into three terms. Contact us or visit the admin office for specific resumption dates.
How do I get a result coupon code? ▼
Visit the VIFF admin office at Emerald Garden Estate, Dakwo District, Lokogoma, Abuja, make the required payment, and collect your unique code. Codes are available Monday–Friday, 8am–2pm.
Do you offer scholarships or bursaries? ▼
Yes. VIFF offers merit-based scholarships to exceptional entrance examination candidates and bursary support for families in need. Please enquire at the bursar's office.
Is there a school bus service? ▼
We operate bus services covering selected areas within Abuja. Please contact the admin office for current routes, schedules, and fees.
