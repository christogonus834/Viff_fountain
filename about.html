Our History

A Legacy Rooted in
Faith Since 2005

VIFF FOUNDATION INTERNATIONAL ACADEMY was founded in 2005 by a group of Christian educators and community leaders with a clear mandate: to establish a school where academic rigour and Christian values go hand in hand.

What began as a small nursery and primary school at Emerald Garden Estate, Dakwo District, Lokogoma, Abuja, has grown into a full-scale international school offering nursery, primary, junior secondary, and senior secondary programmes — all under one roof.

Est. 2005

Key Milestones

Our Journey

2005

Foundation

School founded at Emerald Garden Estate, Dakwo District, Lokogoma, Abuja, with a nursery/primary arm and 120 pioneer students.

2009

Junior Secondary Opens

JS1–JS3 classes added, with dedicated science labs and a computer room.

2013

First WAEC Set

Inaugural SS3 class records a 96% WAEC pass rate — the school's proudest milestone.

2018

New School Block

A new 3-storey block commissioned, increasing capacity and adding a library and assembly hall.

2025

Digital Results Portal

Launch of online result checking system — students can now view results securely using coupon codes.
